This paper introduces C-Eval, a multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. I am a co-author alongside Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, and Junxian He. It was published at NeurIPS 2023.
